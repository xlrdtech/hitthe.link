01The path of one word

you speak→ escalate→ inject→ heard

Escalate cuts whatever is running, inject delivers the word onto an open ear. Nothing may sit on this path. A hook here runs before you are heard, so it can only ever add delay — which is why UserPromptSubmit is dead.

03The events between the events

PreToolUse→ execution — no event→ PostToolUse→ reading & deciding — no event→ next Pre, or Stop

Two gaps have no event and cannot be hooked. Execution time is one; the thinking between a result and the next action is the other. They are where every second of felt latency lives. Nothing can narrate them — they can only be made short, by dispatching work backgrounded and returning instead of waiting inside the turn.

04What EXP is, and is not

  • Is: continuous natural conversation about what is actually happening.
  • Is not: a notification. Never "running Bash", never "tool completed".
  • Speaks on real signal — a finding, a change, a failure.
  • Silent on mechanics and on plain success. Silence is a correct output.
  • Fire-and-forget — detached, so a slow voice call can never become the latency it exists to remove.

05The enforcement, not the intention

Backgrounding cannot depend on discipline — that failed measurably tonight. It belongs on PreToolUse as a gate: a Bash call without background, or one containing sleep, gets blocked and must be re-issued detached. Not overridable, not forgettable.

Same shape as escalate: mechanical, always armed, never a lever anyone taps by hand.
