The Two-Funnel Architecture

Two funnels, not one funnel with tiers. What separates them is a legal firewall, not a conversion step.

“That way the radio show leads doesn’t have a legal conflict.”

L7S does not provide direct credit repair. It sells education, membership, mentorship and systems, and outsources fulfillment to vendors. A broadcast or public-media lead must therefore never land on a paid credit-fulfillment checkout. The free lead magnet is the separation layer that sits between them. Any design that lets a radio listener click straight through to paid credit fulfillment defeats the entire purpose of the split.

01The firewall

Read this diagram first. Everything else on the page is an elaboration of it.

The dashed red path is the one the architecture exists to forbid:
public media → paid credit fulfillment, with nothing in between.

Why the wall is real, not cosmetic
Under 15 U.S.C. §1679a(3)(A)(ii) a business becomes a “credit repair organization” for giving advice or assistance about improving a credit record — not only for performing disputes. §1679b(b) forbids taking payment before services are fully performed. In Georgia, O.C.G.A. §16-9-59 makes operating one a misdemeanor. So the exposure is not created by doing credit repair; it is created by the marketing claim attached to the money. A broadcast audience arriving directly at a paid credit offer is precisely that claim. Source already on file: l7s/OFFER-ARCHITECTURE.md, 2026-08-29.

02What each funnel may and may not touch

The funnel is a container. It holds the pricing/checkout page, the lead magnets, hero calculator links, hitthe.link pages, the PDFs and the roadmap — qi, 18:11:17 and 18:16:48.

Funnel A · free

The lead magnet

Fed by radio, social, public content. Its only job is to convert a stranger into a known contact with a stated reason.
  • May give away real value — PDF, e-book, one-page infographic, calculator
  • May capture name, email, phone into the CRM
  • May run the assessment / quiz and book a call
  • May teach how the credit system works, generally
  • May not take payment of any kind
  • May not promise, imply or price a score outcome
  • May not link directly to paid credit fulfillment
  • May not scope, assess or dispute an individual file
Funnel B · paid

Self-serve checkout

Low-ticket digital products, self-publishing. Entered by choice, not by broadcast referral.
  • May sell completed digital goods, delivered at download
  • May sell membership, education and systems
  • May ascend a buyer into the gated ladder
  • May carry the pricing table and the roadmap
  • May not be the first thing a broadcast lead sees
  • May not collect fees in advance of a regulated service
  • May not market L7S as performing credit repair
  • May not bundle regulated work into a financed package

06Where both funnels land

Verbatim, 18:17:37
“The funnel will lead to the L7S Hero CRM, which is the GoHighLevel… Hero sub-account, until we get our own, until we get a proprietary CRM that’s not under that brand.”

Treat GoHighLevel as interim infrastructure with a stated exit. Three design rules follow from that, and they cost nothing now but are near-impossible to retrofit later:

  • Own the schema, not their objects. Define the contact/lead/opportunity fields as an L7S schema that GHL happens to store. Never let a GHL-only field become load-bearing.
  • Tag funnel-of-origin on every record, permanently. It is the audit trail proving the firewall held. A lead that cannot prove which funnel it came from cannot prove it was not a broadcast lead sold directly.
  • Scheduled export off the platform. Portability that is not exercised is portability that does not exist — the migration must be a re-import, never an extraction project.
