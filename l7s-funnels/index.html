The four things that block a build today

  • No payment processor named. Priority 1 says "including checkout and payment processing" but no processor is chosen. Stripe, GoHighLevel native, or the existing merchant account — this decision gates the entire checkout stage.
  • Digital products are unpriced. The vendor menu prices partners and DWY/DFY, but templates, courses and lead magnets have no numbers. The low-ticket rung of the ladder is empty.
  • No target numbers. Priority 3 asks for "target numbers" — none exist yet, so conversion rates, traffic spend and break-even cannot be computed.
  • Compliance copy is fixed and must ride every page. L7S is not a CRO, lender, broker, law firm or tax advisor; no outcome or score guarantees; regulated services performed by licensed third parties. This is not optional footer text — it governs what the funnel is allowed to claim.

05The BPO model the funnel sells into

Luckie's framing, verbatim: "We still do it our way via our BPO MODEL — Business Professional Outsourcing to our Exclusive Network of Regulated Industry Experts and Specialists." The funnel does not sell a service; it sells coordinated access to a vetted network.

  • L7S HERO S.O.S (OS) — strategic planning playbooks and systems solutions
  • Membership subscriptions — LDE learning environments + BD communities
  • Digital product funnels and upsell verticals
  • IT and accounting staffing — fractional CTOs (devs, techs, engineers) and CFOs (CPAs, CFPs, FP&A agencies, controllers, treasurers)
  • AI agency automation — CRMs, PMPs, ERPs for founders, freelancers, facilitators, financial literacy educators

Agent compensation runs on A.I.R — Actions, Introduce, Refer: send people to the mentorship for a 3-way, they get helped, the agent gets paid. Partnered with McCarthy Law Firm, Rocket Lawyer, and L7S, Inc.

Two operational realities this build must respect

Availability is not continuous. Luckie's own words on why the system exists: "How I'm helping clients while having the ability to compensate for my unavailability due to my Dialysis Schedule." The funnel has to run without him present — that is its actual job, not a nice-to-have.

Timelines cannot be promised. "We aim to expedite where possible, but timelines and outcomes depend on external parties and cannot be guaranteed." Every page in this funnel inherits that constraint.
