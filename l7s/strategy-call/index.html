Before you book

Do you directly provide credit repair?

No. L7S, Inc. is a connection platform linking you with licensed credit professionals and law firms, in full compliance with CROA and FTC regulations. Where a license is required, a licensed professional does that work — we coordinate, educate and match.

What is L7S Hero S.O.S.™?

An AI-powered business coordination platform that connects entrepreneurs and executives with strategic resources through seven specialized service heroes.

What credit monitoring do I need?

IdentityIQ or Score Progression. If you do not have active monitoring, a $1 trial is available during the intake process.

Can the engagement be financed?

Done-for-you engagements can be. A third-party financing company pays L7S at signing and you make payments to that lender on its terms. The credit work is always a separate agreement, never financed, and never paid for until the service has actually been performed. The full structure is on the pricing page →
