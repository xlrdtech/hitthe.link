Row 7 is why this is different

Everyone else in this market stops at "you're funded." A funding consultant hands over a credit line and disappears, and you now have a debt and no plan — which is exactly how people end up worse off than they started.

Campaign Hero is the row where raised capital gets deployed into something that generates the return that services the debt. One coordinated path from "my credit is a mess and I don't know where to go" to "the capital I raised is deployed into a campaign that is paying it back."

The financing path

Done-for-you engagements do not have to be paid out of pocket. Third-party financing turns a five-figure engagement into a monthly payment you can plan around.

You apply

A short application to the third-party financing partner. Separate company, separate decision — not an L7S credit check.

The lender pays L7S

On signing, the financing company funds the engagement upfront. Work starts immediately instead of waiting on a deposit schedule.

You pay the lender

Micro-payments on the lender's terms, direct to them. Your obligation runs to the finance company, not to L7S.

Two agreements, never one

The financed package
  • Cash flow, crypto, forex, business setup and Campaign Hero, plus coordination
  • Lender pays L7S at signing
  • You owe the lender on terms
  • Describes no credit-repair deliverable, prices no credit outcome, promises no score change
The credit agreement
  • Credit work only, standalone and separately signed
  • Paid only after each service is fully performed
  • Monthly in arrears, or per completed deliverable
  • Never financed, never a deposit, never a retainer

Not cross-collateralized · not cross-defaulted · neither agreement conditions the other

"The business build, the setup, and the campaign are financed — the lender pays us and you pay them on terms. The credit work is separate, it's its own agreement, and you don't pay for any of it until it's actually been done."

The sentence we say on every call, because it is the true one.

What L7S is, precisely

L7S, Inc. is an analytical, architectural, AI-powered business strategic planning, coordination and education firm. Three nouns carry the whole business: coordination, education, matching. Not "repair." Not "funding."

L7S does not directly provide credit repair. It is a connection platform that links you with licensed credit professionals and law firms, in compliance with CROA and FTC regulations. There is a licensed professional at every point where a license is required.
