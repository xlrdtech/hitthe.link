Every landing page is a place you move through.

The upload becomes the world. Video or still, it wraps to the inside of a sphere so you stand in it rather than look at it — and scrolling is travel, not paging. Keep scrolling and the world keeps turning; there is no bottom to hit.

Two files

pano.js builds the environment from an upload. x3d.js takes the content sitting in front of it and poses that in space too, so nothing on the page is flat.

<script src="https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js"></script>
<script src="https://hitthe.link/x3d/pano.js" data-src="/media/your-upload.mp4" defer></script>
<script src="https://hitthe.link/x3d/x3d.js" defer></script>

That is the whole integration. data-src takes an mp4, webm, jpg or png.

How the upload is read

  • A 2:1 upload is treated as a true 360° equirectangular panorama.
  • Any other aspect is mirror-wrapped, so it still covers the full turn instead of leaving a dead arc behind you.
  • Video is muted and playsinline — without both, autoplay is refused and the sphere renders black with no error at all.
  • A cross-origin asset needs CORS headers or WebGL silently refuses the texture. Same-origin is the safe path.

Endless scroll

Scroll distance is accumulated rather than read as a position, so reaching the end of the document does not end the journey — the page wraps and the yaw keeps going. Tune it per page with data-speed, data-dolly and data-dim.

Reduced motion keeps the pose and drops the drift. It is never an instruction to go flat.

Running now

The environment behind this page is /diamond-cut/execute-desktop.mp4 — an existing asset, not a stock placeholder. Swap it live with pano.swap('/path/to/other.mp4'), or remove the poses entirely with x3d.off() to see what the page looks like without any of it.
