Desired-state rules - Claude may not edit these and self-certify

  • god-mode-is-canonical-tool-plane - God Mode / SELMCP / mcp.xlrd.org/mcp are ONE tool plane, not three systems.
  • session-o-deprecated - Session O routing is deprecated. No silent fallback into it.
  • xen-context-routing-deprecated - Xen Context routing is deprecated. No silent fallback into it.
  • voice-not-blocked-beyond-five-seconds - Voice must not be blocked beyond five seconds.
  • one-xen-voice - One Xen voice is allowed.
  • no-naive-dedup-on-common-words - Bee/Omi input must not be deduplicated because two messages share an ordinary word.
  • raw-akashic-evidence-remains-available - Summaries never replace raw evidence.
  • lifenote-is-projection-not-source - Lifenote projects Akashic; it is not the source of truth.
  • no-silent-replacement-of-a-proven-path - No new code may silently replace a proven working path without passing its regression tests.
  • claude-is-not-the-sole-authority - Claude may work on Xen. Claude may not be the sole authority deciding whether Xen is correct.

Claims Claude may not make on its own word

working, fixed, live, delivered, deployed, healthy, verified - each needs an independent verifier's receipt.
