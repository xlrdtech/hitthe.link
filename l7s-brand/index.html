The three skins

These are three brands, not three moods. They are never merged on one surface. Each colour below is tagged In use when it was pulled out of an existing L7S artifact, or New when there was no artifact and it had to be chosen.

Light ground is not a preference. East, 2026-08-01, recorded in l7s-site/index.html: “L7S websites must default in light mode.” So HERO's black is a band inside a light page, not the page. That is already how the shipped L7S site treats the mark — a near-black tile behind a gold bolt.

1 — L7S  Luckie's Seven Solutions, Inc. · the parent

Red, white, black. The corporate voice: contracts, the offer architecture, anything signed L7S, INC. Red is the single accent — one red per page, used on the rule, the number chip and the button, nowhere else.

L7S Red In use#d7263dButtons, rules, counters, link text. 4.96:1 on white — passes AA for body text.
Logo Red In use#ef4444Mark only. Named in _shared/l7s-legal-brand-block.md. 3.76:1 — never body text.
Brand Black In use#0a0c10All body text, and the dark bands. Not the page ground.
White In use#ffffffCard and document ground. The third brand colour, carrying real area.
Page Ground In use#f6f7f9Near-white behind the cards, so white panels read as raised.
Muted Ink In use#4f5866Secondary text. 7.4:1 on the ground.

2 — L7S HERO™  the flagship program

Black and gold, black-and-gold lightning mark. This is the $7,000–$21,000 done-for-you room. Gold is metal, not yellow: it never sits on white as text — it sits on black, where it reads 9.4:1. Corners go nearly square (3px) so it reads engraved rather than friendly.

HERO Gold In use#d4af37Named “Gold #D4AF37” in the Hero Card logo SVG. Accent, rules, the bolt.
Void Black In use#0b0b0eNamed “Void Black #0B0B0E” in the same SVG. The HERO ground.
Gold Light In use#f7e08a--gold-secondary from l7shero/index.html. Top stop of the metal gradient.
Gold Deep In use#b9932d--gold-dark, same file. Bottom stop, and the hairline on gold fills.
Gold Ink In use#8a6410The only gold allowed as TEXT on light. 5.0:1. This is what HERO prints as.
Warm Paper In use#f4efe6Body text on the void. Warm white, never pure #fff — pure white buzzes on black.

3 — L7S UNIVERSITY  the academy · American Hero + Scholastic Hero

Primary colours: heroic yellow, red, blue. Red-white-blue is the flag rule at the top of every sheet; navy is the lecture band; yellow is the highlight that marks what the student has to do. Blue leads, red punctuates, yellow never carries text.

Hero Blue In use#1f5cffExisting --brand in l7s-site. Primary action, links, headings.
Hero Red In use#d7263dShared with the parent on purpose — the University is visibly L7S.
Heroic Yellow New#ffc233No prior artifact. A true primary yellow, deliberately not the HERO gold, so the two sub-brands never blur. Fills and highlights only — 1.61:1 on white.
Yellow Ink New#8a5f00Darkened yellow for yellow-coloured TEXT. 5.3:1. Same trick the L7S site already uses for gold.
Scholastic Navy New#10214dDerived by darkening #1f5cff, not a new hue. Body text and the lecture band.
White In use#ffffffThe middle stripe of the flag rule, and the sheet itself.

Spacing, to scale

Radius is a brand signal. L7S 16px, HERO 3px, UNIVERSITY 10px. It is one token — --s-radius — and it changes the personality of every button, chip and card at once without touching a single component rule.

The grid rule. One column below 760px, two above it. Never three — a three-up infographic cell is unreadable on a phone and unreadable on paper at letter size. Depth comes from the band and the KPI strip, not from more columns.

Print & PDF

These pages become PDFs, e-books and printed handouts. Print is a first-class target here, not an afterthought — hit Cmd‑P on this page to see the rules below take effect.

RuleWhy
Page is US Letter, 14mm marginsPrints correctly in the US without “fit to page” shrinking the type.
HERO inverts: void black → white ground, gold → #8a6410 gold inkA black-ground PDF destroys a laser printer’s toner and prints muddy. Gold ink is the AA-passing gold, so the sheet stays HERO without staying black.
Each lead magnet starts on a fresh pageOne magnet, one sheet. That is the unit being handed to a lead.
Cards and callouts lose their tintLight tints band and posterise on most office printers. The 4px rule survives; the wash does not.
Components never split across a pagebreak-inside:avoid on .skin.
Links print their URLA printed page has no hover. a[href^="http"]::after.
Navigation is removed.noprint. Nothing that cannot be clicked on paper is printed.
Body drops to 11pt16px is a screen size; 11pt is a document size.
Slides and decks. The same skins carry into a 16:9 deck without change: the .s-band is the title bar, .s-kpi is the stat row, .s-steps is the agenda, .s-call is the one line per slide that matters. The type scale is already stepped at 1.25× minimum, so a 25px card heading becomes a deck headline by moving up two steps rather than by picking a new size.
E-books. Long-form uses the same tokens with one change: the measure is capped at 64 characters, and .s-band becomes the chapter opener. No new components.
