Your 7-question funnel gap analysis

The checklist from our call. We walk these seven together — each is one thing a book-a-call funnel must answer before it's ready for traffic.

  1. WHO, exactly, is this funnel for?One avatar, named — not "entrepreneurs." L7S has it: F.O.O.D.I.E.S.™. The sharper the WHO, the higher the opt-in.funnel-niche-selection
  2. What is the ONE job?Book the R&R™ call. If a page tries to also sell, teach and list services, it books nothing well.funnel-types-selection
  3. Does the headline follow the formula?WHO · RESULT · OBJECTION · TIMEFRAME, in plain words — selling the outcome, never the "how."funnel-messaging-headlines
  4. Is it one thing, not a website?Nav stripped, one CTA, every extra link removed. Benchmark the opt-in at ≥20% — ≤7% means fix the copy, not the traffic.funnel-messaging-headlines
  5. What warms the lead before the call?The top of the funnel. The 1174 rule says trust is earned over ~11 hours — what content/ads feed this page?funnel-client-acquisition
  6. What's the offer & price ladder behind the call?L7S has it live: $39/mo membership → $97 / $197 / $397 / $1,497 tiers. The call ascends them, itemized not lumped.funnel-offer-pricing
  7. What follows a lead who doesn't book?The tail — the follow-up sequence. The funnel's two gaps are always the top and the tail: nothing feeds it, nothing follows it.ghl-automations / funnel-hacker
