The mechanic
Squeeze the vowels out of the middle, keep the consonant skeleton — SouRCE→SRCE, PHoNE→PHNE, CaNVaS→CNVS, BRoWSer→BRWS. Never truncate (SOUR, PHON) and never abbreviate to initials.
Order
Everything under LIFE is alphabetical, always. A new mark is inserted in place — never appended to the end.
The slash is the seam
Not two words with punctuation between them — one pill cut diagonally. The slash's own lean is the cut angle, so the glyph and the geometry say the same thing, and the glyph can then be dropped entirely.
APPS is the escape hatch
The fifteen are what ships out the box. Anything not defaulted is added under APPS — which is what makes the list finite but complete. The bar for a default is not is it needed, it is is it the first thing you reach for.
Settings is not a mark
It exists, it is never listed. No SETS, no PREF, no CONF. Chrome, not a destination — reachable from anywhere, a door of nowhere.
TIME is both
One axis, both directions. The recorded past and the scheduled future on the same line — scrub back to last year, scroll forward to the appointment. Micro and macro. Timers are a feature of it, not the point of it.
Recognizable on sight
A name that has to be unpacked has already failed, however good the unpacking is. This killed ARES — a reader lands on the war god before they reach Akashic Record Stream, and that beat of confusion is what the test catches.

Open · the name of the whole

The LIFE/XXXX family names the surfaces. The four-letter mark that sits over all of it is still unsettled. ALLN (All In One · Allen, vowel-squeezed) and ARES (Akashic Record Stream) were both self-sourced and both died — ALLN on the ear, ARES on the sight test.

The tension is structural: recognizable-on-sight and clever-in-four-letters pull against each other. Every candidate that passes the sight test is a plain word, and the plain ones — FEED, STRM — were rejected for the whole while STRM survives as a door. A mark can be right for one slot and wrong for the system.

One recorded absence: the ALLN discussion SLXN refers back to is not in the capture log. The name is canon; the referent it points to is pending confirmation. Recorded as missing rather than reconstructed.
