The pattern across every row

Almost nothing here was retired for being slow. Nearly all of it was retired for reporting success while doing nothing — the socket that wrote into a dead relay, the gate that held 62 messages while healthy, the hook registered nowhere, the watcher pointed at an unmounted volume, the sender that committed every retry.

That is why "it's running" is never evidence here, and why every replacement has to assert on an artifact instead.
