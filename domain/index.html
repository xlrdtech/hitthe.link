l7sinc.com — getting it out of Wix

Two years of failed transfers. The cause is public record, and ICANN gives them 5 days.

The diagnosis (verified twice from whois):
Domain Status: clientTransferProhibited
Domain Status: clientUpdateProhibited
Registrar: Wix.Com Ltd. (IANA 3817) · Registry expiry: 2026-08-31

That first flag is a registrar lock. While it is set, the registry rejects every transfer at the protocol level — no number of attempts from Spaceship can succeed. The second flag also blocks nameserver/contact edits, which is why the domain cannot be pointed anywhere either.

Do NOT pay the Wix renewal

Under ICANN’s Transfer Policy, nonpayment for a pending or future registration period is not valid grounds to deny a transfer — only an unpaid previous period is. The failed charge is for the upcoming period, so it cannot block the transfer. An inbound transfer to Spaceship normally adds a registration year, so one action moves it AND renews it. Paying Wix funds the registrar you are leaving and still leaves the transfer undone.

Step 1 — send this once (no negotiation)

From the registrant email of record (xlrdtech@gmail.com). Subject: Formal request under ICANN Transfer Policy — unlock + AuthInfo code for l7sinc.com

I am the registered name holder for l7sinc.com (Registrar: Wix.com Ltd., IANA ID 3817).

This is a formal request under the ICANN Transfer Policy. Within five (5) calendar days of this request, please:

1. Remove the clientTransferProhibited status currently set on l7sinc.com, and
2. Provide the AuthInfo (EPP) authorization code for the domain.

The public whois record currently shows both clientTransferProhibited and clientUpdateProhibited, which blocks transfer at the registry level.

For the avoidance of doubt: under the Transfer Policy, nonpayment for a pending or future registration period is not valid grounds to deny a transfer request. The only outstanding amount relates to the upcoming registration period ending 2026-08-31, not a previous one.

If the unlock and AuthInfo code are not provided within five calendar days, I will file a Transfer Policy complaint with ICANN Contractual Compliance.

Registrant email of record: xlrdtech@gmail.com

Step 2 — if 5 days pass, file with ICANN (this is the lever)

Form: forms.icann.org/en/resources/compliance/complaints/transfer/form

  1. Domain: l7sinc.com
  2. Registrar: Wix.com Ltd. (IANA ID 3817)
  3. Type: Transfer — registrar failed to provide AuthInfo code / failed to remove clientTransferProhibited within 5 calendar days of registrant request
  4. Facts: date of the Step 1 request · whois showing both locks · registry expiry 2026-08-31 · no prior-period debt
  5. Attach: the Step 1 email and the whois output

ICANN compliance contacts the registrar directly. This converts a customer-service loop into a contractual obligation.

Step 3 — transfer at Spaceship

With the lock off and the EPP code in hand, start the inbound transfer. It adds a registration year. Approve the confirmation email promptly — approvals expire.

Do not let this drift inside ~15 days of expiry, where registrars get squeamish and lapse risk rises.

Why it was invisible for two years

Nothing in the Wix UI says “we have locked your domain against transfer.” It just quietly fails, so it reads as your mistake. It was never a competence problem — it was a lock plus a 5-day obligation nobody invoked. The record exonerates.
